Company Overview & Business Model

01

Founded in 2022 by Maor Sason (CEO — previously sold ad-tech company Appush to Magic Software Enterprise) and Roei Barassi (Co-Founder & General Manager), with Shai Bouju as the third co-founder and Chief Innovation Officer.

The 3 Whys

06

Why anything? Complexity scaled with the business — two clouds, an event-streaming layer, a managed database — while observability stayed split across three separate tools. Why Datadog? One dataset across the whole stack, and — for a payments platform — protection on verified money movement, not just user experience.
